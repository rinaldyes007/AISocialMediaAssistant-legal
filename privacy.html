Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

1. Introduction

AISocialMediaAssistant Kantah Sanggau is an internal application designed to assist authorized personnel in managing, reviewing, preparing, and publishing official social media content.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when using the application and its integrations with third-party social media platforms.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features used, the application may process the following information:

  • Application account information used for authentication.
  • Social media account identifiers and platform-related information required to provide authorized publishing functionality.
  • Social media content metadata, such as post identifiers, captions, publication status, and timestamps.
  • Media files submitted for content management and publication.
  • Information required to monitor and manage publication status.
  • Application activity and technical logs required for security, troubleshooting, and system operation.

3. Social Media Platform Data

The application may integrate with third-party platforms including TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube through their official APIs and authorized services.

When a user authorizes an integration, the application may receive information permitted by the relevant platform and the permissions granted by the user.

The application only requests and processes information necessary for the functionality being used.

4. How We Use Information

Information processed by the application may be used to:

  • Authenticate authorized users.
  • Manage social media content.
  • Prepare and review content before publication.
  • Publish approved content to authorized social media accounts.
  • Monitor publication status and errors.
  • Detect duplicate content.
  • Maintain application security and reliability.
  • Troubleshoot technical problems.

5. Content from Third-Party Platforms

The application may process content and metadata obtained through authorized social media integrations for purposes related to content management and authorized reposting workflows.

Processing or republication of third-party content is subject to the applicable permissions, authorization, attribution requirements, platform policies, and applicable laws.

6. Data Sharing

The application does not sell personal information.

Information may be transmitted to third-party social media platforms when necessary to provide an authorized integration or publishing function.

The application may also use infrastructure and service providers required to operate the application, subject to appropriate security and access controls.

7. Authentication Credentials

Authentication credentials, access tokens, client secrets, and other sensitive credentials are intended to be stored and handled securely. Users should not disclose such credentials to unauthorized persons.

Sensitive credentials should not be included in application content, public repositories, or publicly accessible files.

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to operate the application, maintain publication records, support auditing, troubleshoot technical issues, and comply with applicable organizational or legal requirements.

Data that is no longer required may be deleted or removed in accordance with applicable retention procedures.

9. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information processed by the application against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Access to the application and connected social media services is intended to be limited to authorized personnel.

10. Third-Party Services

The application may rely on third-party services and APIs. Those services have their own privacy policies, terms, security practices, and data handling procedures.

Users should review the applicable policies of third-party platforms used through the application.

11. User Rights and Requests

Authorized users may contact the application administrator regarding questions about information processed by the application or requests relating to applicable data protection rights.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to the application, integrations, applicable requirements, or data processing practices.

The latest version will be made available through this page.

13. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of information by AISocialMediaAssistant Kantah Sanggau, please contact the administrator of the application.
